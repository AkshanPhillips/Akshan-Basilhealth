# Payment Methods — NovaVolt Electronics

NovaVolt accepts UPI, all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay, Amex), net banking, and
Cash on Delivery (COD) for orders under ₹15,000. COD carries a ₹29 handling fee.

NovaVolt also supports 3-month and 6-month no-cost EMI on credit cards for orders above ₹5,000, subject
to the issuing bank's eligibility rules — NovaVolt does not control bank-side EMI approval and cannot
guarantee a specific bank will approve EMI for a given customer.

If a payment is deducted but the order shows "Payment Failed," the amount is auto-reversed by the bank
within 5-7 business days. NovaVolt does not hold failed-payment funds and cannot manually speed up a
bank-side reversal.
