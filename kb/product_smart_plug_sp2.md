# Product: NovaVolt SP2 Smart Wi-Fi Plug

Price: ₹899. 10A smart plug, works over 2.4GHz Wi-Fi (does not support 5GHz networks), controlled via
the "NovaVolt Home" app. Supports scheduling, away-mode random on/off, and voice control through
Amazon Alexa and Google Assistant (after linking the NovaVolt Home skill/action).

Max load: 10A / 2200W resistive load. Not rated for inductive loads like large motors or compressors
above 1200W — using it with high-draw appliances beyond rating can trip the plug's internal breaker
repeatedly, which is expected safety behavior, not a defect.

Requires a neutral wire at the socket (standard in most Indian households) — no hub required.
