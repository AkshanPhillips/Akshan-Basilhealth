# Product: NovaVolt PB20000 Power Bank

Price: ₹2,199. 20,000mAh capacity, 22.5W fast charging output (USB-C and USB-A ports, one of each),
18W USB-C PD input for recharging the bank itself (~3.5 hours from empty with a PD charger).

Can charge a typical smartphone roughly 3.5-4 times, or a tablet about 1.5 times, before needing a
recharge — actual numbers vary by device battery health and charging losses, so NovaVolt states these
as typical ranges, not guarantees.

Airline carry-on note: 20,000mAh (≈74Wh) is within the common 100Wh carry-on limit for most airlines,
but passengers should confirm with their specific airline before flying, as rules vary.
