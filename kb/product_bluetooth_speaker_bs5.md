# Product: NovaVolt BS5 Portable Bluetooth Speaker

Price: ₹3,299. 20W output, IPX6 water resistance (safe for rain/splashes, not for submersion), 12-hour
battery life at 60% volume, Bluetooth 5.1 with a 10m typical range (line of sight).

Supports pairing two BS5 speakers together in stereo mode via the NovaVolt Connect app. Has a USB-C
charging port and a 3.5mm aux-in for wired sources.

Floats on water but is not designed to be submerged or used underwater — "IPX6" rates resistance to
powerful water jets, not immersion. Submersion is not covered under warranty.
