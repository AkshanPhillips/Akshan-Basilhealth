# Returns & Refunds Policy — NovaVolt Electronics

Most NovaVolt products are eligible for return within 10 days of delivery, provided the item is unused,
in its original packaging, and all accessories/manuals are included. Earbuds, in-ear accessories, and any
item marked "Final Sale" on the product page are not eligible for return once the seal is broken, for
hygiene reasons.

To start a return, use the "Return/Replace" option in Order History, or ask support with your order ID.
We will send a pickup courier within 2 business days of an approved return request.

Refunds are issued to the original payment method only, after the returned item passes a quality check
at our warehouse (typically 3-5 business days after pickup). Refund processing by the bank/card network
can take an additional 5-7 business days and is outside NovaVolt's control.

NovaVolt support agents (including automated assistants) are not authorized to confirm a refund amount,
waive the 10-day window, or guarantee a specific refund date — only the finance team can confirm a
refund has been issued, via the order's refund status page.
