# Shipping Policy — NovaVolt Electronics

NovaVolt ships from our Bengaluru and Pune warehouses. Standard domestic shipping takes 3-5 business
days and costs ₹79, or is free on orders over ₹1,999. Express shipping (1-2 business days) costs ₹249 and
is available for all pincodes serviceable by our courier partners (Delhivery, Bluedart).

Orders placed before 2 PM IST ship the same day. Orders placed after 2 PM, or on Sundays/public holidays,
ship the next business day. We do not currently offer same-day delivery.

Once an order ships, you'll receive a tracking link by email and SMS. Delivery times are estimates, not
guarantees — monsoon season and remote pincodes can add 1-3 extra days. NovaVolt does not control
courier delays and cannot promise a specific delivery date beyond the estimate shown at checkout.
