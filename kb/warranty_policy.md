# Warranty Policy — NovaVolt Electronics

All NovaVolt-branded products carry a 1-year manufacturer warranty against manufacturing defects,
starting from the delivery date. Power banks and chargers carry a 6-month warranty on the battery cell
specifically (the casing and ports remain covered for 1 year).

Warranty does NOT cover: physical damage (drops, cracks, liquid damage), damage from using a
non-NovaVolt charger/cable, unauthorized repair attempts, or normal cosmetic wear.

To claim warranty service, contact support with your order ID and a description/photo of the issue. If
approved, we offer either a repair (via our service center, 7-10 business days) or a replacement unit,
at NovaVolt's discretion based on stock availability. Warranty claims do not automatically mean a refund;
refunds under warranty are only issued if a replacement/repair is not possible.
