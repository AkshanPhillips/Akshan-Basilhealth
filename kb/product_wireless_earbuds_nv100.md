# Product: NovaVolt NV100 Wireless Earbuds

Price: ₹2,499. Bluetooth 5.3, 6 hours playback per charge (24 hours with the charging case), IPX4 splash
resistance, touch controls for play/pause/skip/volume, built-in mic for calls.

Box contents: NV100 earbuds, charging case, USB-C cable, 3 sizes of ear tips (S/M/L), quick start guide.

Compatibility: works with any Bluetooth 5.0+ device (Android, iOS, Windows, most TVs with Bluetooth).
No app required for basic use; the optional "NovaVolt Connect" app (Android/iOS) unlocks EQ presets
and firmware updates.

Charging case takes ~1.5 hours for a full charge via USB-C; earbuds fast-charge to 1 hour of playback in
10 minutes in the case.
