# Product: NovaVolt PD65 USB-C Laptop Charger

Price: ₹1,799. 65W USB-C Power Delivery charger, compatible with most USB-C laptops (MacBook Air/Pro
up to 14", Dell XPS, HP Spectre, Lenovo ThinkPad with USB-C PD charging) as well as phones/tablets that
support USB-C PD fast charging.

Includes: PD65 adapter, 1.5m braided USB-C to USB-C cable. Input: 100-240V (works worldwide with a
plug adapter, voltage auto-switching). Over-current, over-voltage, and over-heat protection built in.

Note: laptops that charge only via a proprietary barrel connector (older Dell/HP/Lenovo models without
USB-C PD) are NOT compatible with the PD65 — check your laptop's charging port before ordering.
