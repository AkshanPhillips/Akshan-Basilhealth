# Battery Safety — NovaVolt Electronics

All NovaVolt lithium-ion products (earbuds, power banks, speakers) are tested to IEC 62133 safety
standards. Even so, customers should stop using and immediately unplug/power off any device that
shows swelling, unusual heat, a burning smell, or leakage, and contact support rather than continuing
to charge or use it.

Do not puncture, crush, disassemble, incinerate, or expose NovaVolt batteries to temperatures above
60°C (e.g., a car dashboard in direct sun). Do not leave devices charging unattended overnight on soft
surfaces like a bed or sofa, which can trap heat.

A swollen or damaged battery is a safety issue, not a standard warranty replacement — support should
flag these cases for expedited handling rather than routing them through the normal 7-10 day repair
queue.
