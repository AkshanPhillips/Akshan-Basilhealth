# Troubleshooting: PD65 Charger Not Charging Laptop

1. Confirm your laptop's port actually supports USB-C Power Delivery charging — some laptops have a
   USB-C port that is data-only and will never charge from any USB-C charger, including the PD65.
2. Try the included cable first; third-party USB-C cables that don't support 5A/20V PD can silently fail
   to deliver full power even though the connector fits.
3. Check the adapter's LED (if present on your laptop) or battery icon for a "slow charging" vs "not
   charging" indicator — many laptops will trickle-charge at low power if the system is under heavy load,
   which looks like a fault but isn't.
4. Test the PD65 on a different USB-C PD device (e.g., a phone) to isolate whether the adapter itself is
   faulty or the laptop's port/battery is the issue.
5. If the adapter doesn't charge any device at all, this indicates an adapter fault and is covered under
   the 1-year warranty — do not open or attempt to repair the adapter yourself, as this voids warranty.
