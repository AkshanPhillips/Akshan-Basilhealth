# Troubleshooting: NV100 Earbuds Won't Pair

1. Make sure both earbuds are in the case with the lid open, and the LED is blinking white (pairing mode).
   If the LED isn't blinking, press and hold the case button for 5 seconds to force pairing mode.
2. On your phone, forget/remove any existing "NovaVolt NV100" entry from Bluetooth settings before
   re-pairing — a stale pairing record is the most common cause of silent pairing failures.
3. Keep the earbuds within 1m of the phone during the initial pair; move away only after the connection
   is confirmed.
4. If one earbud pairs but not the other, place both back in the case for 10 seconds to resync them, then
   take them out together.
5. If pairing still fails after a full case recharge (case LED should go solid red while charging, then
   off when full), the issue may be hardware-related — this should be escalated for a warranty check
   rather than retried indefinitely.
