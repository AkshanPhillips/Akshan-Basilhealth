# International Shipping — NovaVolt Electronics

NovaVolt currently ships internationally to Nepal, Sri Lanka, UAE, and Singapore only. International
orders take 10-18 business days and are shipped DDU (Delivery Duty Unpaid) — the recipient is
responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged on arrival, which NovaVolt cannot predict
or pre-pay on the customer's behalf.

Power banks (like the PB20000) cannot be shipped internationally by air due to lithium battery air
freight restrictions; international orders containing a power bank will have that item automatically
removed with a refund for that line item, and the rest of the order shipped normally.

NovaVolt does not currently ship outside the four countries listed above.
